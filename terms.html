Overview

BirdID Lite is a free educational bird identification quiz created to help users learn about birds, conservation, habitats, and trusted birding resources.

Use of the App

You may use BirdID Lite for personal, educational, and non-commercial purposes. Please do not misuse the app, attempt to disrupt it, copy it for misleading purposes, or use it in a way that violates applicable laws or platform rules.

No Professional Advice

BirdID Lite is for general education and enjoyment. Bird identification information, conservation notes, and links to outside resources are provided for learning purposes and should not be treated as professional, scientific, legal, financial, or conservation management advice.

Third-Party Resources

BirdID Lite may link to outside resources such as eBird, Audubon, Wikimedia Commons, conservation groups, and other educational sources. We are not responsible for the content, policies, or availability of third-party websites.

Images and Credits

Bird images and other media may be used under public domain, Creative Commons, or other permitted licenses. Attribution is provided on the Credits page where applicable.

Pi Network and Payments

BirdID Lite is free to use during launch. Optional Pi donations may be added in a future version after payment testing is complete. No payment is required to use the quiz at this time.

Conservation Donations

BirdID Lite intends to support bird conservation when verified nonprofit bird conservation organizations are able to accept Pi. Any future public donation records will be shown on the Public Donations Ledger.

Changes to the App

We may update, pause, change, or discontinue BirdID Lite at any time, including quiz content, features, links, donation options, or availability.
